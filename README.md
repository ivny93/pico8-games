# pico8-games
Collection of pico-8 games I made

## SN8

https://www.lexaloffle.com/bbs/?tid=156344

A rendition of the classic Snake 2 on Nokia!

### Controls:
- ARROWS: move the snake
- Z/X: Select the level on the main manu

### Gameplay
Eat the red fruits, avoid walls and your own tail.
The more you eat, the faster you get.
Sometimes a golden fruit will appear for a brief moment: eat it to get 3x points!

### Levels:
- Classic: there are no walls, you can warp from one end of the level to the opposite side
- Tunnel: Walls will appear on the top and bottom side of the level, you can warp only horizontally
- Box: You're surrounded by walls, you cannot warp
- Maze: Navigate the maze, but watch out for the spots where you can actually warp: the grass on the field can give you a hint ;)
